Flows are the automations you build in CodeZero. Each flow starts with a trigger and then runs the nodes connected to it.
In this guide we'll build a small REST endpoint: a flow that answers a `GET` request to `/ping` with the text `pong`.

<Callout type="info">
  **Prerequisite:** You have a project to build the flow in. If you don't have one yet, follow the [Creating a project](./quick_creating_a_project.mdx) tutorial first.
</Callout>

---

<Steps>
<Step>

## Open your project

On the home dashboard, click on the workspace that contains your project.

![CodeZero](../../../public/tutorials/quick-creating-a-flow/cZ_01_openWorkspace.png)

Then click on the project in the **Projects** list.

![CodeZero](../../../public/tutorials/quick-creating-a-flow/cZ_02_openProject.png)

</Step>
<Step>

## Create a new flow

In the **Explorer** on the left, click **Create workflow**.

![CodeZero](../../../public/tutorials/quick-creating-a-flow/cZ_03_clickCreateWorkflow.png)

Choose the trigger that starts your flow. For a REST endpoint, select **Webhook**.

![CodeZero](../../../public/tutorials/quick-creating-a-flow/cZ_04_selectWebhookTrigger.png)

Give your flow a name. Only alphanumeric names are allowed, for example `ping`.

![CodeZero](../../../public/tutorials/quick-creating-a-flow/cZ_05_enterFlowName.png)

Click **Yes, create!** to create the flow.

![CodeZero](../../../public/tutorials/quick-creating-a-flow/cZ_06_clickCreate.png)

</Step>
<Step>

## Open your new flow

A **Created flow** message confirms the new flow, and it now appears in the **Explorer**. Click on it to open it.

![CodeZero](../../../public/tutorials/quick-creating-a-flow/cZ_07_openYourNewFlow.png)

</Step>
<Step>

## Open the edit panel

The flow opens in the flow editor and only contains the Webhook trigger so far. Click the pencil button in the top right to open the edit panel.

![CodeZero](../../../public/tutorials/quick-creating-a-flow/cZ_08_openEditPanel.png)

Then click on the **Webhook** node to show its settings in the panel.

![CodeZero](../../../public/tutorials/quick-creating-a-flow/cZ_09_selectWebhookNode.png)

</Step>
<Step>

## Configure the webhook

In the **URL** field, enter the path your flow should listen on, for example `/ping`.

![CodeZero](../../../public/tutorials/quick-creating-a-flow/cZ_10_enterUrl.png)

Open the **Method** dropdown

![CodeZero](../../../public/tutorials/quick-creating-a-flow/cZ_11_openMethodDropdown.png)

and select the HTTP method the flow should react to, here **GET**.

![CodeZero](../../../public/tutorials/quick-creating-a-flow/cZ_12_selectGet.png)

</Step>
<Step>

## Add a Respond node

With the Webhook node still selected, click **Add node** in the toolbar at the bottom.

![CodeZero](../../../public/tutorials/quick-creating-a-flow/cZ_13_clickAddNode.png)

Search for `respond`

![CodeZero](../../../public/tutorials/quick-creating-a-flow/cZ_14_searchRespond.png)

and select **Respond**. This node sends the HTTP response back to whoever called your endpoint.

![CodeZero](../../../public/tutorials/quick-creating-a-flow/cZ_15_selectRespond.png)

</Step>
<Step>

## Configure the response

The Respond node is now connected to the Webhook node, and its parameters are shown in the panel.
Fields that still need a value are marked with an error until you fill them out.

Enter `200` as the **HTTP Status Code**.

![CodeZero](../../../public/tutorials/quick-creating-a-flow/cZ_16_enterStatusCode.png)

For the **Content Type**, click the suggestions button next to the field

![CodeZero](../../../public/tutorials/quick-creating-a-flow/cZ_17_openContentTypeSuggestions.png)

and select `text/plain`.

![CodeZero](../../../public/tutorials/quick-creating-a-flow/cZ_18_selectTextPlain.png)

Finally, enter the text to send back as the **Response Payload**, here `pong`.

![CodeZero](../../../public/tutorials/quick-creating-a-flow/cZ_19_enterResponsePayload.png)

<Callout type="info">
  For `application/json` the payload must be an object. For all other content types, a plain string is expected.
</Callout>

</Step>
<Step>

## Save your flow

Click **Unsaved. Save now** at the top of the editor to save your flow.

![CodeZero](../../../public/tutorials/quick-creating-a-flow/cZ_20_saveFlow.png)

<Callout type="info">
  Changes are also saved automatically within one minute.
</Callout>

</Step>
<Step>

## Set the project slug

The project slug is part of the URL of your endpoint, so give it a readable value.
Click on the project name at the bottom left of the sidebar to open the project menu.

![CodeZero](../../../public/tutorials/quick-creating-a-flow/cZ_21_openProjectMenu.png)

Click **Settings**.

![CodeZero](../../../public/tutorials/quick-creating-a-flow/cZ_22_clickSettings.png)

Enter a **Slug** for your project, for example `my-test-project`.

![CodeZero](../../../public/tutorials/quick-creating-a-flow/cZ_23_enterSlug.png)

Click **Save changes** to apply it.

![CodeZero](../../../public/tutorials/quick-creating-a-flow/cZ_24_saveSlug.png)

</Step>
<Step>

## Call your endpoint

Your endpoint is now live. Its URL is built from three parts:

```text
<REST server>/<project slug>/<URL of the flow>
```

- **REST server**: the address of the CodeZero REST server, for example `https://rest-cloud.codezero.build`
- **Project slug**: the slug from the project settings, here `my-test-project`
- **URL of the flow**: the path you entered in the Webhook node, here `/ping`

Together, that gives `https://rest-cloud.codezero.build/my-test-project/ping`.
Send a `GET` request to it, for example with Postman, curl or simply your browser. The flow answers with status `200 OK` and the text `pong`.

![CodeZero](../../../public/tutorials/quick-creating-a-flow/cZ_25_testWithPostman.png)

</Step>
</Steps>
